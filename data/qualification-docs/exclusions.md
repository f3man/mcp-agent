# Exclusions

## Excluded categories

The company does not bid on construction, heavy machinery, or civil-engineering tenders — it
holds no relevant licenses or certifications for these categories, and past attempts to bundle
them with core categories (see past-bid-postmortems.md, 2021 entry) resulted in disqualification.
It also does not bid on tenders primarily for software development/custom software licensing —
"IT services" in the company's profile means hardware supply, network setup, and maintenance,
not bespoke software.

## Regions not served

The company has no local presence, warehouse, or subcontractor network outside Kyiv, Kyiv
Oblast, and Lviv Oblast. Tenders based in other regions would require third-party logistics
arrangements that haven't been costed or qualified, and should not be treated as automatically
serviceable even if the category and value otherwise match.

## Contract value ceiling

The company deliberately does not pursue single contracts above UAH 2,000,000 — this is a
capacity decision, not a certification gap. A tender above this ceiling should be treated as a
mismatch regardless of category or region fit.
