# Past bid postmortems

## 2024 — Kyiv municipal hospital IT services (won)

Won a UAH 800,000 contract to supply and maintain desktop/network IT equipment for a Kyiv
municipal hospital. Won primarily on delivery timeline (2 weeks faster than the next bidder) and
a clean ISO 9001 quality-management submission. Lesson: for IT services tenders in our regions,
a tight, credible delivery schedule is a bigger differentiator than price alone.

## 2023 — regional medical supplies contract (lost, price)

Lost a medical-supplies tender to a lower-cost competitor. Our bid was technically compliant but
priced roughly 18% above the winning bid. Lesson: the company's medical-supplies margin structure
does not compete well below a UAH 1,200,000 contract threshold — below that, cost-focused
competitors reliably underbid us, so tenders in that range and category need an explicit
bid/no-bid judgment call rather than an automatic bid.

## 2022 — office equipment framework agreement (won)

Won a 2-year office equipment framework agreement with a regional administration by leaning on
past-contract references and the local SME registration, despite not being the lowest bidder.
Lesson: framework/multi-year agreements weight track record and local presence more heavily than
one-off tenders do.

## 2021 — construction-adjacent office renovation supply (lost, disqualified)

Disqualified from a tender that bundled office furniture supply with minor renovation work,
because the eligibility criteria required a construction-sector license the company doesn't
hold. Lesson: watch for tenders that bundle our core categories with adjacent work outside our
certifications — these should be flagged as likely ineligible even when the headline category
looks like a match.
