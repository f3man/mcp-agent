# Financial capacity

## Annual turnover

Example Industrial Supply LLC's audited annual turnover is approximately UAH 15,000,000. This
comfortably supports the company's stated project value range of UAH 50,000–2,000,000 per
contract — turnover-based eligibility thresholds in tenders are typically expressed as a small
multiple of the single contract value, and the company clears that bar for any tender within its
normal range.

## Bank guarantee / bid security capacity

The company can provide a bid security (bank guarantee or deposit) of up to UAH 300,000 without
requiring additional collateral arrangements. Tenders that require bid security above this
amount need a case-by-case decision with the bank and should be flagged for human review rather
than assumed feasible.

## Payment terms tolerance

The company can tolerate standard public-sector payment terms (30-60 days post-delivery) without
cash-flow strain for contracts up to its stated maximum project value. It cannot comfortably
service contracts that require the supplier to pre-finance more than 90 days of delivery before
first payment — this is a financial capacity blocker, not just a preference.
