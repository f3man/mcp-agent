# Certifications

## ISO 9001

Example Industrial Supply LLC holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management,
covering the supply of IT services, office equipment, and medical supplies. The certificate is
valid through March 2027 and is renewed annually via a third-party audit. It does not cover
construction, installation, or maintenance services — only the supply and logistics chain for
the three categories above.

## Local SME registration

The company is registered as a small/medium enterprise (SME) with the Kyiv Oblast state
registrar. This registration is frequently listed as a qualifying or scoring criterion in
tenders that reserve a portion of the contract value for local SMEs, but it does not, by itself,
satisfy category-specific technical certification requirements — those are handled separately
(see the ISO 9001 entry above).

## No specialized medical-device certifications

The company is **not** certified for the supply of Class IIb/III medical devices (per the
relevant national medical-device regulation) — its medical-supplies certification only covers
Class I/IIa consumables (dressings, PPE, basic diagnostic supplies). Tenders requiring a
manufacturer's medical-device conformity certificate for higher device classes are outside the
company's qualification.
